# Currency rules

- Base currency: **USD**. Add the currency of the user's country (asked in phase 0 or detected from the answers). Add a third only if the product expands there.
- The rate comes only from the central bank of the user's country (or of the currency's issuer), with the **date**. If you cannot open the source, say so and give the amounts in the original currency only.
- Prices are quoted in the currency they were published in, with the date. Convert once, at the end, at one dated rate. Never mix a price in roubles with a market in tenge.
- A price older than 24 months or without a date is grade C.
